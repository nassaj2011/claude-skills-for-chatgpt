| Invariant | Enforcement | Negative test |
|---|---|---|
| At most one booking per slot | Database `UNIQUE(slot)` | Two concurrent reservations for the same slot |

Race/failure matrix: concurrent reservations must yield one insert and one conflict; duplicate callbacks must yield one state transition and one no-op/conflict; a crash before commit must leave neither a booking nor payment transition durable, while a crash after commit must be safely retriable through idempotency.

Migration sequence: expand schema with the required state/payment-event fields and indexes; backfill/validate existing data; deploy mixed-version-compatible code; then enforce non-null/state-transition requirements once validation is complete. Gate it on backup, rollback plan, and lock-impact review.
